Hobbies & Interests

Outside of my professional life, I enjoy activities that inspire creativity, personal growth, and community engagement. I have a passion for photography, capturing everyday moments and landscapes with a storytelling approach. I’m also an avid reader, especially of historical fiction and leadership development books, which help me stay curious and continuously learning.

In my free time, I enjoy hiking and nature walks, which keep me refreshed and balanced. I also love volunteering at local animal shelters and participating in community outreach programs, where I find fulfillment in giving back and connecting with others.

These hobbies not only bring joy but also help cultivate patience, discipline, and empathy—traits that positively influence my personal and professional life.
